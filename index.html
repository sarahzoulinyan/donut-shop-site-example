Our Journey

Every step was taken with love and dedication

2020

Where It All Began

Donut Delight began in a small kitchen with a dream: to bring happiness through delicious donuts.

2021

First Store

We opened our first store on Paulista Avenue and won the city over with freshly made donuts.

2022

National Expansion

We opened locations in Rio de Janeiro and Belo Horizonte, bringing our unique flavors to more people.

2023

Innovation and Sustainability

We launched sustainable packaging and exclusive new recipes that quickly became favorites.

2024

National Recognition

A leading food guide named us the country’s best donut chain, a milestone in our journey.
